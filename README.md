# The Chemistry of Us — The Second Quest

A bespoke, point-and-click puzzle adventure built as the grand finale for the last door of a homemade advent calendar. Hosted on Neocities (`thesecondquest.neocities.org`), it translates a real-world love story—and a real-world laboratory—into an interactive game-within-a-game.

## 🧪 Overview & Premise

Step into Research Facility 09, a retro-styled chemistry laboratory where an active Alchemist is investigating a classified experiment: Subject A + Subject B = ?. 

With attraction detected and a chemical bond confirmed stable over a 9-year duration, the final formula remains locked. To uncover it, the player must explore the lab, collect specimens, and earn a special coin that unlocks an entire second adventure game hidden inside.

## ✨ Key Features

A Game Inside a Game: Solve the laboratory puzzles to earn a token that grants access to a full secondary adventure game.
Point-and-Click Lab Exploration: Inspect chemical vials, a microscope, classified researcher identification dossiers, a locked specimen cabinet, and a retro CRT television.
VHS Archive Puzzle: Test tapes from the Research Archive - VHS Collection (A Field in England, Spartacus, A Christmas Story 2, and 2001: A Space Odyssey) in the VCR to find the right clue.
Evidence Tracking: Track progress (`0 / 4`) as you uncover the four pieces of evidence hidden around the room.
Custom Retro Aesthetic: Warm, atmospheric browser-based art designed to feel like stepping into a secret late-night experiment.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open [thesecondquest.neocities.org](https://thesecondquest.neocities.org/) in any desktop web browser. Does not work on mobile.
2. Click around the laboratory to examine equipment, read classified notes, and test VHS tapes in the CRT TV.
3. Gather all 4 pieces of evidence to earn your coin and unlock the second quest.

## 🛠️ Built With

HTML5 / CSS3 / Vanilla JavaScript — Lightweight, dependency-free static web architecture.
Neocities — Static web hosting.
